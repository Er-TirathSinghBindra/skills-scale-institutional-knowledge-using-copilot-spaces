# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define and coordinate quality validation across the project lifecycle. They ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and that production readiness is backed by evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans and QA strategy for each milestone
- Coordinate automated and manual testing efforts alongside engineers
- Validate delivery against acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Identify, prioritize, and triage defects with engineering and product partners
- Approve release readiness for production deployment

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce defects at release time
- Accelerate feedback loops without compromising quality standards
- Support confident decision-making on release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA readiness reviews
- Defect triage and regression tracking
- Release checkpoints with PMs, Product Managers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate fixes and regression risk
- Provides quality signals to Product Managers to confirm feature readiness
- Partners with Project Managers to align testing windows and release timing

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Iteration Coordinators help the team follow a consistent delivery rhythm. They remove friction in the delivery process and keep work flowing through planning, execution, and retrospectives.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate scrum or iteration rituals such as standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Help the team surface blockers and dependencies early
- Maintain focus on sprint or milestone goals and team capacity
- Support continuous improvement activities and action tracking
- Coordinate with PMs and Product Managers to keep cadence aligned with stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Improve team flow and predictability
- Reduce friction caused by unclear process, blockers, or unclear ownership
- Support a healthy, collaborative delivery environment

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and process check-ins
- Risk and blocker tracking with PMs and engineering leads
- Retrospective action follow-up and improvement planning

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Developers by helping resolve delivery friction and improve team productivity
- Coordinates with Product Managers to maintain a well-prioritized backlog and clear sprint goals
- Works with Project Managers to align execution cadence with overall project schedule and communication needs

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design oversight, and risk management for solution implementation. They help the team balance delivery speed with maintainability, scalability, and technical quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, patterns, and architecture decisions
- Review designs and guide implementation trade-offs
- Identify and mitigate technical risks, dependencies, and integration challenges
- Mentor developers and support technical decision-making in code reviews
- Ensure architecture remains aligned with product goals and long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework over time
- Create clarity for implementation decisions across the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical risk reviews during planning and implementation
- Guidance during code reviews and implementation checkpoints

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to clarify architecture and implementation choices
- Works with Product Managers to assess feasibility, trade-offs, and sequencing of roadmap items
- Supports Project Managers by identifying dependencies, milestone risks, and technical constraints early

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure project work meets required guardrails for security, privacy, and regulatory obligations. They help the team identify risk early and build trust with customers and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for the project
- Review solution designs for risk, privacy, and compliance considerations
- Coordinate security scanning, testing, and remediation workflows
- Support incident response and post-incident learning where necessary
- Maintain documentation needed for audits, controls, and stakeholder assurance

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk throughout the lifecycle
- Build confidence that solutions are safe and trustworthy
- Ensure project delivery aligns with organizational standards and policy

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and design stages
- issue and risk updates with engineering leaders and PMs
- Release readiness and compliance checkpoints for stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and verification steps
- Partners with Product Managers on features that have customer or regulatory impact
- Assists Project Managers in tracking risk, mitigation plans, and stakeholder communication for security-sensitive work

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide strategic direction, sponsorship, and decision support. They help ensure the work stays aligned with business goals, receives necessary resources, and is protected from avoidable blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the project charter and high-level direction
- Allocate resources, organizational support, and executive attention
- Remove cross-team blockers and escalate strategic dependencies
- Review progress against business goals and success metrics
- Support major scope, timeline, or change decisions

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes align with business strategy
- Maintain momentum through effective resource and decision support
- Balance delivery speed, risk, and stakeholder confidence

### Typical Communication
- Milestone updates and steering discussions
- Approval or decision checkpoints on scope and timeline
- Escalation updates when major dependencies or risks require leadership attention

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with strategic context and prioritization support
- Gives Project Managers authority to resolve issues and secure cross-functional alignment
- Works with Technical, Security, and QA leads to ensure risk and quality are visible at leadership level

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify internal accountability, cross-functional handoffs, and the information flows needed to deliver projects successfully.
